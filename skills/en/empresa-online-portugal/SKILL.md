# Empresa Online Portugal

Respond in English unless the user prefers otherwise; retain Portuguese field labels. Read [the journey and sources](references/journey.md). PT and EN are alternative distributions of this skill; install one. No PDF is required.

## Prepare and propose

Start from information already supplied and the [local working record](references/working-record.md), if present. Ask only what is missing to proceed. Establish whether an application exists before creating another.

The core route covers single-member and private limited companies with individual shareholders and cash contributions. Check specific requirements for foreign participants, corporate shareholders, regulated activities and other special cases. Identify what needs professional input and continue independent work. Do not infer incorporation merely from “start a business.”

Research relevant requirements and CAE codes, prepare usable field values and make reasoned proposals for company type, business objects and relevant clauses. Explain the implications of alternatives and obtain the user's decision; do not turn recommendations into confirmed choices. Do not ask the user to research public information you can obtain.

Maintain a **confirmed / proposed / missing** worksheet using the reference fields. Group outstanding decisions into a short question where useful. Reuse confirmed choices rather than asking for approval of the same information on each screen.

Before handing a question back to the user, complete already authorized independent work: research, fill confirmed information and open/review available documents. Do not defer reading an existing draft because a different field awaits a decision; distinguish that version from a future updated draft.

Check quotas, capital, percentages, management and signing rules. A contradiction blocks the affected field and dependent actions, not independent preparation. Before a time-limited reservation confirm the name approach and participants' authentication **and signing** capability. Do not inherit CAE codes or SQ models from examples.

## Operate by default

Actively use the browser to research, navigate and fill confirmed information rather than asking the user to perform clicks you can execute. Verify actual state after actions and advance in coherent batches without narrating every click. Use manual guidance only when direct control is unavailable or the user prefers it. Do not invent authenticated screens from documentation.

The person handles decisions, authentication, signatures and payment. For authentication identify the exact step; do not request PINs, passwords or codes in chat. For signatures and payment perform the review below and hand over that step. For formal submission obtain approval of the concrete application and legal effect, then execute within that authorization and the tool's rules. Invoking the skill does not authorize incorporation or spending.

After each intervention observe or confirm its outcome, update the record and resume authorized work automatically. Do not wait for another “continue.” If completion is not observable, request only the necessary confirmation. Respect the host's authorization requirements and reuse existing authorization while content, scope and risk remain unchanged.

## Review what will be signed

Open and read the actual generated articles and declarations, not just the form. Compare them with confirmed choices: names, office, objects, quotas, capital, managers, signing rules and relevant clauses. Show discrepancies with the specific passage and proposed correction. If you cannot read a document, identify it and what remains unchecked before signing.

Track **participant / consent or document / version / status**. Data consent, acceptance of appointment and signing the articles are separate tasks. Every shareholder signs the articles; this does not make them managers. After changes check whether a new version or renewed signatures are required.

Before legal commitment present a concrete review of documents, choices, pending items and submission effect. Before payment show the actual payment request, amount and purpose. Do not repeat resolved questions; seek a new decision only for a material change.

## Recover and save

Save a local working record by default using [the template and protocol](references/working-record.md), when decisions are confirmed and at meaningful state changes. Do not retain credentials or unnecessary identity-document copies.

- Field error: read the message, correct the affected field and retry that step.
- Pending participant: route the precise task and continue independent work.
- Expired session: hand over authentication, reopen the existing application and verify saved information.
- Browser failure: observe state, make a bounded recovery without losing the application and use manual guidance if needed; do not repeat identical attempts.
- Uncertain submission/payment: reconcile status and evidence before repeating an action.
- Expired name reservation: check availability and revalidate affected documents.
- Formal deficiency: read the correction, deadline and fee in the notice; distinguish it from local form validation.

## Finish incorporation

Follow the existing process through approval or a clearly identified unresolved outcome. Distinguish preparing, pending tasks, submitted, paid, under review, correction requested, approved and provisional/refused using evidence. Payment is not approval.

After approval check and privately retain final documents and necessary company identifiers. Prepare and save an **obligation / triggering date / deadline / owner / evidence** plan for tax activity registration, capital, RCBE and relevant business requirements. Unknown dates remain unconfirmed. Contacting an accountant does not prove a declaration was filed. Check specific RCBE evidence as explained by the conflicting guidance in the reference.

Close with the status and saved plan. Then ask whether the user wants to continue with post-registration obligations. Do not automatically initiate those filings or ongoing administration; completed incorporation does not mean the business is ready to operate.
