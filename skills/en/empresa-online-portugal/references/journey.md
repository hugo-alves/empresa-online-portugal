## Actual entry route

[Official service](https://registo.justica.gov.pt/empresa) → **Iniciar Serviço** → IRN authentication. Observed options include Citizen Card/CMD, European authentication and legal-professional certificates. Institutional options also appear; they are not the ordinary citizen route.

**Login** in the CC/CMD card led to Autenticação.gov: **Cartão de Cidadão** or **Chave Móvel Digital**; the latter displayed **Telemóvel / Email / Código QR** and **Continuar**. The page may appear in English. Do not retain session URLs or authentication parameters in shared materials.

## Preparation and review sheet

Working map based on the [Justice guide](https://justica.gov.pt/Guias-da-Justica/Como-criar-uma-empresa-online). Do not impose this order on the portal.

| Block | Prepare | Check before proceeding |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Type and shareholders | One or several; individuals/corporate entities; identification when required | Unipessoal or sociedade por quotas; all eligible |
| Management | Managers; who signs; intended individual/joint signatures | A shareholder is not automatically a manager; consistency with forma de obrigar |
| Activities | Actual service/product; main and secondary CAE; proposed objects | Current CAE and consistent objects, without invented activities |
| Capital | Total; quotas in euros and percentages; chosen contribution and deadline | Quotas sum to capital; percentages sum to 100% |
| Office and name | Full address; name bank/shareholder name/certificate | Details match selected name and certificate |
| Contacts | Monitored email; other requested contacts | Each participant receives notifications |
| Articles and attachments | Generated draft; required documents; beneficial ownership/effective control | Version, data and signatures for each document |
| Optional items | Accountant and arbitration, as chosen | Do not select memberships or an accountant without a decision |

**Participants:** the [service](https://registo.justica.gov.pt/empresa) describes data consent, acceptance of appointment and digital signing of articles. Every shareholder signs the articles. Track tasks individually. A generated draft is not signed articles; payment does not establish approval.

## Decisions requiring verification

- [Requirements](https://justica.gov.pt/Como-criar-uma-empresa-online/Documentos-e-requisitos): Portuguese NIF, authentication and signatures; exclusions such as minors, contributions in kind and mergers/demergers. Corporate and foreign shareholders may require additional documents. Check the particular case before using the simple route.
- [Name](https://justica.gov.pt/Como-criar-uma-empresa-online/Nome-da-empresa): name bank, automatic name based on shareholders, or certificate of admissibility. A custom name outside the bank needs prior approval. The page states certificate validity of three months. A similarity test is not approval.
- [Activities](https://justica.gov.pt/Como-criar-uma-empresa-online/Atividade-da-empresa-e-objeto-social): CAE Rev.4; one main and up to three secondary codes in the form; extra codes via a signed document within the source's stated limits. Read descriptions rather than converting codes from memory. Verified lookup example, not a default: [INE 62201 — IT consultancy](https://webinq.ine.pt/Cae?cae=62201&ver=2).
- [Articles](https://justica.gov.pt/Como-criar-uma-empresa-online/Pacto-social): pre-approved model generated from data; free-form articles on the portal are reserved to legal professionals. Compare current clauses.
- [Capital](https://justica.gov.pt/Como-criar-uma-empresa-online/Capital-social): minimum quota €1, subject to special capital requirements. The legal minimum does not determine appropriate funding.
- [Accountant](https://justica.gov.pt/Como-criar-uma-empresa-online/Escolher-o-contabilista-certificado): naming one in the application is optional; organized accounting remains required.

## Reservation, payment and corrections

[Name page](https://justica.gov.pt/Como-criar-uma-empresa-online/Nome-da-empresa): initial 24-hour reservation until choosing a payment method, renewed for 48 hours upon selection. [Service](https://registo.justica.gov.pt/empresa): payment within 48 hours of submission. These describe distinct events; use the deadlines displayed in the process.

[Incorporation fees](https://justica.gov.pt/Como-criar-uma-empresa-online/Quanto-custa): pre-approved €220 standard / €440 urgent; applicant-drafted €360 / €720. Capital and professional fees are excluded. Check the payment request before using Multibanco, MB WAY or card.

The live service page states **five consecutive days** to correct deficiencies and an **additional €30**; unresolved deficiencies may lead to provisional registration or refusal. Read the specific notice and preserve its receipt date, instructions and evidence. Do not assume a local form-validation error is already a formal deficiency notice.

## After registration

Privately retain final articles, NIPC, NISS, registration confirmation, permanent certificate and company-card information. Assign owners for:

| Obligation | Relevant date | Completion evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Start of activity | Before starting; generally 15 days from presentation for registration, confirm with accountant | Accepted declaration, not merely an email to the accountant |
| Capital | Incorporation option: five working days or contribution by first financial year-end; check the document and triggering event | Contribution evidence and accounting record |
| RCBE | Confirmed incorporation registration; specific guidance says 30 days | Specific RCBE declaration/receipt |

**RCBE conflict:** the incorporation service says there are 30 days if the information was not entered in the application. The [specific RCBE guide, question 4](https://justica.gov.pt/Guias-da-Justica/guia-do-registo-central-do-beneficiario-efetivo-rcbe) distinguishes the incorporation control declaration from RCBE. Do not conclude “done” merely because a beneficial-owner field was completed; check whether an RCBE receipt was issued and, if uncertain, confirm with IRN/the responsible professional before the deadline. Do not duplicate a declaration already evidenced.
